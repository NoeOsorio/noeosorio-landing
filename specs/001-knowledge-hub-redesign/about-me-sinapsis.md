# About me: Sinapsis (Noe's concept, 2026-11-15)

## The idea in Noe's words, summarized

- About me is not a window. The whole Noe OS screen becomes a space the visitor moves through at will (scroll, arrows, swipe), forward and back, and it loops. It is not a video.
- It starts with chaos: Noe's head. The chaos settles into **four galaxies**, the four areas his mind is split into: **psychology, business, content creation and computer science**. Infrastructure is part of computer science, never a separate world (Noe, 2026-11-15).
- **All four are real galaxies.** Different real types (spiral, elliptical, barred spiral, ring), different colors, but all galaxies. Never cubes, grids, drawings or icons.
- **When the camera focuses on a galaxy, its own stars start moving and rearrange themselves into the scene of that part of the story.** The scene is made of that galaxy's stars, in true 3D, and it is built live in front of the visitor.
- Each scene has its own camera angle and movement (zoom, low angle, side, high). The visitor can only look around inside that frame.
- Slowly the galaxies connect, and from their connection new things are created.
- No floating words at all: no labels, no names, no orbiting topics. The only text is the one subtitle sentence of each scene.
- Quality bar: high production quality. It must feel 3D, alive and like something is being created, never flat dots or badly drawn shapes.

## Approved galaxy look (Noe, 2026-11-15)

- References chosen by Noe: NGC 1300 for computer science, M51 (Whirlpool) for business, M104 (Sombrero) for psychology, Hoag's Object for content creation. Images by ESA/Hubble and NASA, CC BY 4.0: the site must credit them.
- Technique: each real photo is placed in 3D on its galaxy's disk (background removed, round soft fade), with hundreds of thousands of live stars on top for glow, depth, pointer reaction and, later, rearranging into scenes. Formula-made galaxies were rejected ("glue with glitter").
- Live test: https://claude.ai/artifact/79jTV2hMGGt8sXxdNgFyXT (version 6).

## Scenes (draft, real content only)

1. Chaos. "My head is chaos."
2. Four galaxies. "Inside it live worlds that seem to have nothing in common."
3. Camera dives into the people galaxy; its stars rearrange into people talking. "I like to listen to people."
4. Camera moves to the business galaxy; its stars form a small shop whose door is broken; the visitor finds it. "The real problem is usually where nobody is looking."
5. The business galaxy again: the door is fixed, the shop lights up, a point of sale glows inside (Pyventa, a small detail, never the center) and a 3D chart rises beside it. "I love understanding how a business works and how it can grow." (Earlier line "I study psychology to understand how small businesses hire" was false and was removed with the scene built on it.)
6. The algorithms galaxy; its stars form a network that flows into a light bulb over a person. "I mix computer science with understanding why people do what they do."
7. The infrastructure galaxy; its stars form a container ship that sails to the shop. "At Porter I learn every day why good infrastructure holds it all up."
8. Pull back: the four galaxies, now joined by bridges of stars. "I study psychology because I want what I build to matter."
9. Everything becomes the hexagon logo. "That chaos becomes things I create to help others."

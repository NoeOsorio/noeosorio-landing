# Quickstart: verify the new home

1. `npm ci` (once), then `npm run build` and `npx tsc --noEmit`: both clean.
2. `npx vite preview --port 4318`: open http://localhost:4318/.
3. Hero: wait 16 s, the LED logo is complete; hover three stars; click the logo.
4. Wallet: open two projects, follow their actions.
5. Passport: scroll through every page.
6. Learning: pull the lever three times; filter the list.
7. Tips: choose a tip (thank-you, then the tip jar); choose No tip (judged sequence).
8. Robots: activate "I'm a robot" with the keyboard; Open raw shows /llms.txt.
9. Footer: Back to top, Privacy, Cookie settings reopen consent.
10. Repeat at 768 and 390 px; no horizontal scroll, no console errors.
11. Go to About and back ten times; no extra loops.
12. Compare each section's screenshot with its prototype in `prototypes/`.
